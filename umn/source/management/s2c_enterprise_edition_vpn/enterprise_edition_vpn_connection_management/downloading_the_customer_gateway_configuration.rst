Downloading the Customer Gateway Configuration
==============================================

Scenario
--------

After creating a VPN connection, you can download and view the customer gateway configuration.

Notes and Constraints
---------------------

VPN connections that use SM series cryptographic algorithms do not support the download of customer gateway configurations.

Procedure
---------

#. Log in to the management console.

#. Click |image1| in the upper left corner and select the desired region and project.

#. Click |image2| in the upper left corner, and choose **Network** > **Virtual Private Network**.

#. In the navigation pane on the left, choose **Virtual Private Network** > **Enterprise - VPN Connections**.

#. On the **VPN Connection** page, locate the target VPN connection, and choose **More** > **Download Customer Gateway Configuration** in the **Operation** column to download the customer gateway configuration.

   In the **Download Customer Gateway Configuration** dialog box, you can export device configurations by specifying the device vendor, series, model, and version.
